## 14. Mastery Rules

### Level 0 — Unseen
Never meaningfully attempted.

### Level 1 — Attempted
Made a serious attempt, even if unsuccessful.

### Level 2 — Solved With Help
Required a hint, partial guidance, or the complete solution.

### Level 3 — Independently Solved
Solved without external help.

### Level 4 — Explained
Can explain the solution, invariant, correctness, complexity, and edge cases clearly.

### Level 5 — Pattern Mastery
Can recognize the underlying pattern and apply it to a new variation without relying on the memorized solution.
